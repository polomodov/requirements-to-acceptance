# Dossier of one delivery

All roles, documents, events and amounts are fictional. The date of a record determines when the team could have used it. The `stable` status refers to the record itself. Who approved a commitment and what exactly was accepted is stated inside the document.

| Point in time the context is needed for | Records already available |
|---|---|
| End of 6 October 2026 | [I-01 and I-02. Commitment, authority and the sales request](intake.md) |
| 7 October, before the scope and schedule meeting | The previous records and [E-01. Experiment log and assignee estimates](prototype.md) |
| 7 October, after the meeting | The previous records, [D-01. The choice and two approvals](decision.md), [R-01. Version 2](requirement.md) |
| 21 October, before the acceptance decision | The previous records and [T-01–T-04. Observations on build b17](observations.md) |
| 21 October, after the product owner's decision | All records, including [A-01. Build acceptance record](acceptance.md) |

For an exercise, give the participant the files available at the chosen moment. For a retrospective, start from a specific observation in the acceptance log and follow the links to the requirement and the decision.
